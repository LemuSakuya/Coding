# HUNNU Background Cover Edit Prompt

Use case: compositing
Asset type: campus recruiting magazine-cover poster

Primary request:
Edit the provided Hunan Normal University campus background photo into an original campus recruiting magazine-cover poster. Keep the provided image as the real photographic background, then add one 2D anime-style pink-haired schoolgirl in the foreground and clean editorial cover typography.

Style reference direction:
Use the feeling of a cinematic campus magazine cover: real university photography, dramatic sky or golden-hour lighting, quiet youth-campus atmosphere, elegant white editorial typography, small side-column cover lines, and a calm new-member recruitment mood.

Character reference direction:
The character should feel like a soft pink-haired anime schoolgirl: gentle eyes, loose wavy pink hair or side ponytail, pink bow or cat-ear-like hair accessory, school-uniform-inspired outfit, delicate clean linework, cel-shaded anime colors, soft highlights, and a calm inviting expression.

Scene/backdrop:
Preserve the provided Hunan Normal University background as a real photograph. Keep the buildings, sky, pavement, trees, signs, pedestrians, vehicles, perspective, and lighting realistic. Do not convert the background into anime or illustration.

Subject:
Add one full-body or three-quarter-body 2D anime girl naturally placed in the foreground, standing or leaning where the photo has visual space. She should look intentionally illustrated against the real 3D campus environment, like a mixed-media recruiting poster.

Typography:
Add minimal magazine-cover text. Main title: "HUNNU JOIN". Subtitle: "New Members 2026". Small accent text: "Explore / Resonance / Rebirth". Optional Chinese text: "湖南师大 招新季". Use clean white or soft off-white editorial typography, placed in open sky/wall areas. Keep text legible and uncluttered.

Lighting/mood:
Cinematic campus atmosphere, warm sunset or dramatic daylight matching the background, hopeful new-semester recruitment feeling, subtle rim light on the anime character if appropriate while keeping her flat 2D anime style.

Constraints:
Change only by adding the anime character and editorial poster text. Preserve the original photo background realism and major campus landmarks. Do not copy any existing game title, reference-image title, social media handle, watermark, QR code, or logo. Do not write "WUTHERING WAVES". No cosplay, no realistic human texture, no photorealistic character, no full anime background.

Avoid:
Extra characters, distorted face or hands, unreadable text clutter, fake watermarks, trademark logos, QR codes, heavy overpainting, turning the campus background into anime, blocking important campus signage more than necessary.
